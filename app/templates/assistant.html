Use Sewa Setu with an AI assistant

An AI assistant that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), such as Claude or ChatGPT, can help a citizen apply for the Old Age Pension and follow it up through this official channel. The portal keeps the official record and applies exactly the same rules as the counter and the website.

For citizens, families and CSC operators

Add this connector (server URL) to the assistant:

{{ citizen_url }}
  • When the assistant connects, Sewa Setu asks for the applicant's mobile number and an OTP on this website. The assistant never sees the OTP. The verified mobile number becomes the application's contact number.
  • The assistant asks for the details, gives a link to photograph the age-proof document on the phone, reads everything back, and files the application only after the applicant says yes.
  • Applying through an assistant is being piloted in: {{ pilot|join(', ') }}. Anyone can check the status of an application.

For block officers

{{ officer_url }}

Sign in with your departmental login. Your assistant sees the pending cases of your blocks, most urgent first, opens a case with automatic checks, shows the age-proof document, and records your decision under your name with your reason. It cannot approve an application that breaks a scheme rule, and it decides one case at a time.

Privacy and shared phones

Details shared in a conversation are seen by the assistant and the company that runs it. A sign-in lasts at most 2 hours for a citizen. On a shared phone or kiosk, ask the assistant to sign out when you finish, or end the sign-in from the status portal under Connected applications.
